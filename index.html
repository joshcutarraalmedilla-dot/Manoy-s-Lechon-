How to Order

Ordering your lechon is quick and easy. Just follow these 4 simple steps.

01
📋

Choose Your Lechon

Pick between a whole lechon (perfect for big fiestas) or lechon belly (great for smaller gatherings). Use our Price Checker to see the cost by weight.

02
💬

Message us on Facebook or call/Viber our number. Tell us the type, weight, your preferred date, and if you need delivery or pick-up.

Pick up your order or have it delivered within Bohol. Serve it fresh and hot — crispy skin, juicy meat, pure Bohol flavor!
